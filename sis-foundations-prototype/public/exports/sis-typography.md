Adham approved all ten roles and weights 400, 500, 600, and 700.

| page-title | 28px | 600 | 36px | 40px |
| section-heading | 20px | 600 | 28px | 32px |
| subsection-heading | 18px | 600 | 26px | 30px |
| body | 16px | 400 | 24px | 28px |
| field-label | 14px | 500 | 20px | 24px |
| help | 14px | 400 | 20px | 24px |
| button | 14px | 500 | 20px | 24px |
| navigation | 14px | 500 | 24px | 24px |
| table-heading | 14px | 500 | 20px | 24px |

Compact density keeps text sizes. All generated numbers use international digits 0 through 9.
The prototype serves all four IBM weights locally. Other families and French examples remain in the historical archive.

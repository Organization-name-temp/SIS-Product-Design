SIS component direction

Three variations of one test surface: buttons, fields, badges, cards, a dialog, and a data table. All three use the approved foundations only — E-JUST red #EA232B, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, the canonical token files. Nothing here invents colors. You pick the direction; it becomes the component library’s default face. The variations flex shape, elevation, and density — not brand.

Variation 1 — Contract

By the book. The current approved foundations, untouched: 8px control radius, 12px panels, flat surfaces, 1px borders, tokens as written. The safe baseline and today’s workbench face.
